## Completion language

The implementation can be presented as a substantial delivered website and connected platform with documented engineering depth. Full customer-journey launch certification was held in the latest reviewed assessment. This showcase does not convert configured capabilities, local candidates, historical checks, or illustrative screens into a claim of production acceptance.

No performance score, lead-conversion improvement, uptime percentage, time-saved estimate, accessibility conformance, or financial return is claimed without measurement.

## Relationship to PowerPortals

305UGC is the company-specific website and deployment case study. PowerPortals is the shared platform/product showcase. Shared-platform demo screens are labeled as such and do not claim to show current live 305 customer or staff data.
