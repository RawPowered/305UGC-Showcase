# 305UGC public-site preservation

A screenshot-led record of the public 305 Underground Construction website, captured on **October 25, 2026** while the live pages were available.

This is a visual preservation archive, not a website mirror or source-code backup. It records the rendered public experience and the URL inventory advertised by the page, post, and portfolio sitemaps.

## Capture record

- **58 public content URLs:** 26 site pages, 13 articles and guides, and 19 portfolio pages.
- **Desktop:** full-page JPEG captures at a 1440-pixel viewport width.
- **Mobile:** full-page JPEG captures at 390 × 844 viewport dimensions for nine key visitor paths.
- Images and web fonts were allowed to load before capture. The original cookie-consent state was left untouched.
- Pages were viewed anonymously. No forms were filled or submitted, and no account or CRM workflow was opened.
- Screenshots show the page as rendered at capture time. They do not guarantee that links, forms, APIs, or hosted content will remain available.
